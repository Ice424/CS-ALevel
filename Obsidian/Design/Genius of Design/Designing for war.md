## German design in the war
before the war German goods were seen as cheap


werkbundarchiv - Deutscher Werkbund
an institute commited to redefine German design
- 1 designer deigned most products for AEG including the typeface, branding and factories
these committees called for standardisation and deigning for function

### volkswagen beetle
"there is no product that is as complex and as simple to use as a car"
the beetle was designed for function and quality with adequate room inside the car and an overall streamlined design

the Werkbund's intrest in an object's identity caused the Nazis to use the beetle as a symbol during election campaigns
it was the birth of corporate identity

## British design in the war

they had been kicked out of Germany and were in need of a sub machine gun

### The Sten Gun
while the German's used high quality materials and expert machining the British didn't have the materials or the workforce so they had to improvise 

the mk1 used basic off the shelf components & bed springs in its making

the mk2 cost £2 7 shillings and stripped back some of the wood and further simplified the design

it changed British design from Victorian style handmade to designing of mass production

a toy designer simplified the design even further
