### Safe Working Practices

Health and safety precautions set guidelines that protect employees
- training
- machine maintenance
- Extraction Systems
- PPE
- Accident reporting

### Health and Safety Act 1974

H&S at work act - all employers must ensure that all employees and visitors to the workplace are protected in terms of health and safety

The key term 'as far as reasonably practice' means employers can cut corners or be just below the guidelines

### Safe Working Practices in Industry

When there is moment around factory there should be designates safe zones
machinery must be fitted with flashing lights and signals to alert of movement

job rotations must also be used so workers are not harmed from repetitive strain injury 


### Safety Symbols

Any action that is carried out in advanced that protects against injury is a precaution

Symbols can show how the precautions should be made or that they are made

### Risk Assessment
A risk assessment is a document that takes into account any risk in the workplace, it considers the level of risk and suggests what precautions should be taken

Employers that have five or more employees are required to do a risk assessment

Risks can't always be removed but they need to be known

### Consumer Protection Act

Helps safeguard consumers from products that do not reach a reasonable level of safety
