
## Antistatics
- Prevents build yo if electrical charge by attracting moisture from the surroundings

## Flame Retardants
- Bromine, chlorine, phosphorous, or metals are added to polymers to reduce the risk of combustion

## Plasticises
- Added to plastics to make them less brittle and hard at room temperature

## Fillers
- Provide bulk to the product meaning less polymer is used
	- sawdust, wood flour, chalk, clay, talc
- or to improve thermal conductivity
	- mineral fillers
	- Heat and coll down faster increasing mould cycle times


## Antioxidants
- reduce degradation from oxygen

## UV Light Stabilisers
- Prevents discolouration due to UV light


## Biodegradable Plasticisers
- Make plastics softer more flexible and easier to break down 

## Bio-Batch Additives

- Oxy-Degradable
- Photodegradable
- Hydro-Degradable
