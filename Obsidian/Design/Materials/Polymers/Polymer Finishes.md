
Most plastics are self finishing
any texture comes from the finish of the moulds used to from them or the die profiles used when extruding


## Adding Pigments
- Adding colour is often done for aesthetic appeal 
- Pigments are usually fine powders which are added to the clear granules

#### Aesthetics
- Polymers can be coloured using pigments paints and gel coats
- Products can be enhanced using overmoulding


## Thermochromic Pigments/ Photochromic
- temperature sensitive pigments
- application include plastic cutlery, bowls, and cups

## Phosphorescent Pigments
- Store light energy and release later in the dark
- "glow in the dark"

## Hydrographic Printing
- Colour images are printed onto water soluble film which floats in a tank


### Electroplating
- Plastics are covered in a conductive layer or etched before plating with a metal


### Rubberising spray
- A slightly textured coating with a mat finish can be sprayed onto various materials to provide grip

### Polishing 
- plastic can become rough or scratched when processed and can bevome weathered or faded if left outside
- they are usually used to restore a high quality finish
