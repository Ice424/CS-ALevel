
## Press forming
- metals such as low carbon steel and aluminum are used
- common in mass production or large scale patch production due to the cost of producing dies
- dies are made from hardened steel
- press forming can be done hot or cold
- cold will be stronger in the long term but an break easily in the forming process

## Spinning
- spinning is a way of forming sheet metal giving radial symmetry
- typically used in mass and batch production often with cnc lathes
- metal is pushed onto a mandrel(former) with a roller

## Cupping and deep drawing
- cupping and deep drawing are used to from tube shapes
- fire extinguisher and aerosol cans
- deep drawing is when the depth of pressing exceeds he diameter
- high set up cost as a hydraulic press is needed

## Drop forging
- used to shape hot metal
- spanner, pliers and screwdriver shafts.
- hot metal is forced into a shape under great pressure
- it is suitable for mass production however dies can be changed quickly

## Wrought iron forging
- wrought iron has low carbon content making it easy to shape
- "traditional" forging
- traditional fences

## Bending

- a press brake is used for bending a sheet of metal
- a brake holds the metal and the punch is lowered into place

## Rolling
- Rolling is used to reduce the thickness of metals
- typically used to make the stock forms
- can be done hot or cold
- cold rolling will have finer tolerances but with more stress on the metal

## Die casting

- die casting is used with lower melting point metal sch as aluminum, pewter & zinc
- tool steel is used for the reusable dies
- the complexity and cost of making the dies makes it ideal for mass production

	- gravity casting
	- high pressure die casting

## Sand casting

- sand casting is used to from high melting point metals
- carried out in foundries using molds made of "green" sand
- it is a slow process usually used for one off or batch items as the molds are single use

## Investment casting
- is used to cast items with awkward shapes
- high quality finish
- a wax copy is made and then coated with ceramic. the wax is melted away and metal is poured while the mold is still hot
