### Tungsten Carbide
- Made from ceramic and metal known as cermet
- Used in cutting and abrasion tools
- Is extremely hard rigged and impact resistant
- level 9 on Mohs hardness scale
### Carbon Fibre Reinforced Plastic
- Carbon fibre is thin lightweight and has high tensile strength
- Fibres are woven into fabric the more complex the weave the tougher the fabric
- Layers of carbon fibre are impregnated with a plastic resin to make carbon fibre reinforced plastic (CFRP)
- used in bikes, F1, planes
- chemical resistant & lightweight
### Glass Reinforced Plastic
- commonly known as Fibreglass
- Combined with thermosetting plastics such as polyester or epoxy
- glass fibres are strong in tensions with little compressive strength
- Is a cheaper alternative to carbon fibre
- resistant to UV and corrosion
- Watertight so good for sea vessels

### Aluminium Composite Board
- A sheet material combining a polymer core faced with a thin sheet of aluminium
- Used of signage and architectural cladding and wall panels
- can be used for high quality UV printing
- easy to form and cut, stable, durable, lightweight, and resists corrosion

### Fibre Cement
- A composite building material used for cladding roofing and facades
- Originally made form asbestos fibres that are now replaced with cellulose and synthetic fibres
- Durable lightweight resistant to rust weathering and chemicals and insect attack
- Is vapour permeable, reduces any condensation in buildings but also inculcates noise and heat
- Has a smooth and minimalistic finish

### Concrete
- A versatile composite can be cast into moulds for vertical structures or poured to create flooring and paving
- Concrete is heavy durable and has a high compressive strength and is not weaken by moisture or mould
- Low cost low maintenance fire resistant and can be polished
### Reinforced Concrete
- Steel rebar reinforced concrete has improved strength under tension
- Taller stronger and complex forms can be made
- other materials can be used
