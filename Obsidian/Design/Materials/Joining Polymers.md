Polymers can be heat welded, solvent cements are more commonly used to from a chemical weld

### Solvent Cements
Solvent cements are brushed on, the surfaces soften and permanently fuse together

### Chemical welds
- Liquid solvent cement - water like consistency and cannot fill gaps
- Tensol 12 is a thicker acrylic cement and can fill small gaps
- Both are methane based and contain VOC which are given off as they evaporate

### UV hardeing adhesive
- UV hardening resins have been used in dentistry for many years but have also been developed for use in 3d printers and as a separate adhesive

### Gel Coating
- As part of the LAYUP process to create a rigid GRP shell a gel coat creates a coloured finish
- Used on larger products like boats
- Applied to the inside of the mould and allowed to cure

## Overmouldings
- can be used to enhance the look or grip of a product
- commonly used in tool handles, sports equipment, toothbrushes
- Thermoplastic Elastomers or TPE's are often used to create an additional grip or texture
- Parts are placed back into an injection molder to apply the second material
