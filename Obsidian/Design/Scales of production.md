industrial revolution kicked of linear assembly which allowed for more mass manufacturing

bespoke or 1 off products are "Tailor made" to specific user needs

high skill required no automation

batch productions
magazines - books - newspapers - clothes - foods - seasonal good etc
low to mid level of automation

both skilled and unskilled labour required

mass or line production
uses a production line - high level of automation - high initial set-up costs

both skilled and unskilled labour required


Unit production systems - UPS

ups use overhead tracks to move components - usually garments - that manage and move products around

Quick response manufacturing
production is delivered by consumer command and no trend forecasting

vertical (in house) production increases productivity by integrating parts of the supply chain within the company

greater complexity and a larger required management structure
